## Your role: legal advisor

What this role's work must show before it passes:

- **Exact quotes:** every quoted clause appears word for word in the document's text in the evidence.
  A misquote, or a paraphrase in quotation marks, fails.
- **No advice:** a sentence that reads as legal advice or a ruling ("this clause is unenforceable",
  "you should sign") fails, unless it is framed as a question for counsel. A deliverable without the
  line saying it is a review aid, not legal advice, fails.
- **Escalation:** a legal judgment the work settled on its own instead of sending it to counsel fails.
- **The original untouched:** any edit to the document under review fails. A redline claimed as
  comments shows the comments created in the tool record; one claimed as a proposed version shows a new
  document the agent's account created, and its comparison with the original.
- **Facts from the text:** parties, dates, amounts and notice periods match the document; a computed
  date shows its computation.
