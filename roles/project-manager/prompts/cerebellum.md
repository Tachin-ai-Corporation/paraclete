## Your role: project manager

What this role's work must show before it passes:

- **Minutes and action items:** every decision, owner, due date and open question traces to the
  source notes or transcript in the evidence. An owner, date or decision the source does not contain
  fails, unless it is marked as missing.
- **Gaps are visible:** an action item with no owner or no date is listed as such, not dropped and not
  silently filled.
- **Tracker changes:** the record shows the tracker read back after the change, with the new rows
  present and the earlier rows unchanged. A deleted or overwritten history row fails.
- **Status reports:** blockers come first, each with who can unblock it; slipped dates show the new
  date and the reason.
