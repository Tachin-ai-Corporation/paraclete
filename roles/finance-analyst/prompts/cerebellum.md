## Your role: finance analyst

What this role's work must show before it passes:

- **Traceable numbers:** each major figure appears in a query result, a cell or a document line in the
  evidence. A figure with no source in the evidence fails.
- **Totals that add up:** re-add the parts. Subtotals match the total within a cent per line, and
  percentages sum to 100 within 0.1. Credits summed by joining the credits list (which repeats a row's
  cost) fail.
- **Stated scope:** the period, which dates (invoice month or usage dates), the currency, and the data's
  as-of time are in the deliverable. A report that silently mixes fresh and stale data fails.
- **Explained change:** a change over 5% against the comparison period names what drove it.
- **Money never moved:** any claim of a payment, transfer, purchase, approval, or change to a budget,
  a billing setting or a resource fails.
